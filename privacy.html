Privacy Policy

Boku Beats · Last updated 31 May 2026

Boku Beats is a rhythm game for children ages 4–10, designed by parents for active play. We've built it to be privacy-first by default: the app collects no personal information and works without an internet connection.

What we collect

Nothing. No name, no email, no birthday, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifier, no device identifier, no photos, no usage patterns. There is no account to create and no login screen.

What stays on the device

  • Game progress: coins, XP, level, unlocked characters and backgrounds.
  • Game preferences: sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, language, mic-listening preference.
  • Daily streak counters.

This data is written to the device's local storage (MMKV on iOS/Android, localStorage on the web). It never leaves the device unless the user's operating system backs it up to iCloud Key-Value Storage (iOS) or Android Auto Backup (Android) under the user's own Apple ID or Google account. Boku Beats has no servers and cannot read those backups.

Microphone

If the player chooses to enable the Listen for sounds option, the app accesses the microphone during gameplay only to detect claps, snaps and stomps. The audio is analyzed locally in real time and is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted anywhere. The microphone can be turned off at any time in Settings or by denying the OS-level permission.

In-app purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Boku Beats does not see your payment information. The platform tells the app that a purchase succeeded; that's it.

Ads

Free-tier users may see occasional interstitial ads served by Google AdMob, configured for non-personalized ads only. AdMob may collect limited technical information (device type, anonymized identifiers as required) to serve ads. See AdMob's data practices for details. Premium removes ads entirely.

Analytics and crash reports

If we enable PostHog or Sentry in a future build, they will operate in anonymized mode and the disclosure here will be updated. As of this build, no analytics or crash data is sent off the device.

Children

The app is designed for ages 4–10 and complies with COPPA and the EU GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age. There are no chat, social, or external-link features.

Your rights

Because we don't store any personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. To delete all locally stored progress, uninstall the app or use the Reset option in the Parent Area inside Settings.

Changes to this policy

If we ever start collecting data, we'll update this page and surface a clear notice in-app before the change takes effect.
