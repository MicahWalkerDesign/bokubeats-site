How a round works.

Each round takes about a minute. Long enough to play, short enough to play again.

1

Pick a challenge

Easy, Medium, or Hard. Toggle the microphone on if you want it to listen — off if you just want to watch.

2

Learn the moves

A short legend shows the moves for today's round — claps, stomps, fruit symbols, whatever the difficulty uses.

3

Watch the beat

Boku plays a short rhythm. Each note moves across a line. Watch carefully — that's the sequence to copy.

4

Play it back

The line replays empty. Clap, snap, or stomp on each note. The phone listens, scores hits and combos, awards stars.

Common questions.

Quick answers for grown-ups before they hand the phone over.

Does it actually record my child's voice?

No. The microphone runs only during a round, only listens for short percussive sounds (claps, snaps, stomps), and never saves or uploads any audio. You can also turn the mic off and play in watch-only mode.

How old does a child need to be?

Boku Beats is rated 4+. Kids 4–6 do best on Easy with a grown-up nearby. Kids 7+ can usually drive Medium and Hard on their own.

Are there ads in the free version?

There's an occasional short ad between rounds in Free. Premium removes all ads. There are no ads aimed at children and no third-party trackers.

Will it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once installed, the entire game plays offline. The only thing that needs a connection is restoring a Premium purchase on a new device.

How is my data handled?

Boku Beats doesn't collect personal data. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics on by default. See the Privacy Policy for the full plain-English version.

I have a problem with the app — what do I do?

Email support@bokubeats.app with a quick description and a screenshot if you can. A human will reply.
